# Material experience gaps

Check the relevant evidence ledger and controlling employer records before labeling a gap. Distinguish direct evidence, transferable evidence with its boundary, unknown or undocumented work, confirmed lack of direct experience, and experience with unresolved scope or disclosure. Prior applications and writing examples are not factual authorities.

Ask one focused neutral question at a time about unresolved work likely to change fit or positioning. Describe actual actions, decisions, scope, artifacts, or observed outcomes. Do not imply that the user must have done the work. Reuse existing durable answers. If the user declines or proceeds without answering, keep the unknown and continue with supported claims.

Summarize newly supported evidence, transferable matches, confirmed gaps, unanswered questions, and disclosure limits outside employer-facing wording. For each material unresolved qualification, identify required versus preferred status, the evidence boundary, and the practical application implication. Screening risk is judgment, not employer feedback. Stretch applications remain the user's choice.

Record confirmed facts and disclosure status in private evidence records. Keep role-specific conclusions in the matching review. Factual confirmation does not approve disclosure or final wording. Do not rewrite approved, produced, or submitted history as a side effect. Do not interview every broad-search lead.
